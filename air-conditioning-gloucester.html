Air Conditioning Installation in Gloucester

MJ Maintenance supplies, installs and services air conditioning systems across Gloucester and Gloucestershire — for homes, home offices, salons, and commercial spaces. Matt handles the full job himself: survey, supply, installation, and ongoing servicing with no subcontractors in the chain.

What we cover

  • Split system air conditioning — supply and install
  • Multi-room systems for larger properties
  • Home office and bedroom installations
  • Commercial and salon installs
  • Annual servicing and filter maintenance
  • Fault diagnosis and repair

Cooling and heating in one unit

Modern split-system air conditioning units do both — cool in summer and heat efficiently in winter. They're significantly cheaper to run than electric panel heaters, and because they move heat rather than generate it, they're far more energy-efficient. Many of our customers install primarily for summer cooling and end up using the heating function year-round.

Survey first, no surprises

Before any installation, Matt visits to assess the space — wall access, cable runs, drainage routes, and the right unit size for the room. You get an accurate quote based on what's actually there, not a ballpark figure that changes on the day. No hidden extras once the job is underway.
