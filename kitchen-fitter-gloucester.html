Kitchen Fitting in Gloucester

MJ Maintenance fits kitchens across Gloucester and Gloucestershire — from supplying and fitting a new unit run to a full redesign of your kitchen layout. Matt handles the entire job: carpentry, plumbing, tiling, and electrics all in the right sequence with one person accountable from start to finish.

What we cover

  • Full kitchen installation from flat-pack or rigid units
  • Worktop supply and fit — solid wood, laminate, quartz
  • Sink and tap installation and plumbing
  • Appliance plumbing — dishwashers, washing machines
  • Splashback tiling
  • Under-cabinet and feature lighting
  • Layout changes and wall removals

Why single-trade kitchen fits fail

A kitchen installation touches plumbing, electrics, carpentry and tiling — usually in a tight sequence where one trade has to wait for another. When you hire a separate person for each trade, you spend your time coordinating diaries and chasing people. When Matt does it all himself, there are no gaps. The job runs to a schedule you're given up front, and if anything needs adjusting mid-job, there's one person to call.

Supply or fit only

Already have your units? Matt can fit what you supply. Planning to buy and need help choosing? He can advise on what works best for your space and budget. Either way, you get a proper job done by someone who takes pride in the finish.
