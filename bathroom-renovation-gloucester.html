One Point of Contact for the Whole Job

Most bathroom renovations go wrong at the joins between trades — the plasterer arrives before first-fix plumbing is done, the tiler's booked while the adhesive is still wet, the electrician can't come until the walls are ready. When you have separate contractors, you become the project manager by default.

MJ Maintenance handles every part of a bathroom renovation directly — plumbing, electrics, plastering, tiling and fitting. That means the sequence is right, the timeline is realistic, and there's one person to call if anything needs attention. You deal with us from the first conversation to the day we hand back your keys.

  • All trades handled in-house — no subcontractors
  • Correct sequence every time — no waiting for other trades
  • One realistic timeline from the start
  • One person accountable from quote to completion

Tiling in Gloucester — Walls, Floors & Wet Rooms

Tiling is one of the parts of a bathroom renovation that shows most when it's done wrong. Inconsistent grout lines, tiles that aren't level, or adhesive that wasn't right for the substrate — these are the things that come back. We tile to last, using the right preparation for each surface.

  • Bathroom wall tiling — large format and standard tiles
  • Floor tiling — porcelain, ceramic and natural stone
  • Wet room tanking and tiling
  • Shower enclosure and tray tiling
  • Feature walls and decorative layouts
  • Kitchen splashbacks
