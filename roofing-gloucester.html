Roofing Services in Gloucester

Whether you've got a slipped tile, a flat roof that's seen better days, or you need a full roof replacement, MJ Maintenance handles it all across Gloucester and Gloucestershire. Matt works hands-on on every job — no subcontractors, no call centres, just one accountable person who takes pride in doing it right.

What we cover

  • Tile and slate repairs and replacements
  • Flat roof replacements and modifications
  • Ridge and hip re-pointing
  • Flashing repairs around chimneys and walls
  • Gutter clearing, repairs and replacement
  • Roof inspections and condition reports

Why get your roof sorted sooner rather than later

A small problem left through a Gloucestershire winter rarely stays small. A single slipped tile lets water into the loft, soaks the insulation and starts working on the timbers. By spring, what was a £100 repair has become a much larger job. Getting a proper look done while the weather is dry means small faults get caught before they cost you.

What to expect

Matt will give you an honest assessment of what needs doing, what can wait, and what's fine to leave. No inflated scare stories, no invented problems. Just a straight answer and a fair price — with no job too small to be worth his time.
