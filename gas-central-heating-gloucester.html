What we do

Gas & Heating Services in Gloucester

Boiler Replacement

Old or unreliable boiler swapped for a new one, with the system cleaned and left running properly.

Boiler Moves

Boiler, pipework and flue moved to a new position, often as part of a kitchen renovation or extension.

Boiler Repairs & Servicing

Faults looked at and regular servicing to help keep your boiler safe and reliable.

System Conversions

Older systems with a hot water cylinder and loft tanks converted to a combi boiler, with the old tanks and redundant pipework taken out.

Radiators & Pipework

Radiators replaced or added and heating pipework altered or extended, including system filters to help protect your boiler.

Landlord Gas Safety

Annual gas safety checks and certificates for rental properties, plus any repairs they turn up.
