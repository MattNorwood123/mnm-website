Landscaping in Gloucester

MJ Maintenance takes on landscaping and outdoor builds across Gloucester and Gloucestershire — from a new patio to a full garden room or driveway. Matt works on every job himself, which means you get a consistent finish and one person who stays accountable from the first spade to the final clean-up.

What we cover

  • Patio laying — porcelain, natural stone, block paving
  • Driveway installation and edging
  • Garden rooms and outdoor studios
  • Raised beds and garden walls
  • Fencing and gate installation
  • Drainage and groundworks
  • Decking — composite and timber

Built to last

Outdoor work that fails almost always fails at the base — poor groundwork, inadequate drainage, or a sub-base that wasn't compacted properly. Matt prepares every job as if the ground underneath matters as much as the surface on top, because it does. A patio fitted right should still be level and looking good ten years from now.

Outdoor as an extension of indoor

The best outdoor projects don't feel like an afterthought — they feel like a continuation of the house. Whether you want a low-maintenance space for the family or a proper garden room with electrics and insulation, MJ Maintenance can plan and build it as a coherent project rather than a patchwork of separate jobs.
