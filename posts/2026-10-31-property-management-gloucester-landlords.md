## Void periods and changeovers

Every week a property sits empty costs you rent. Because we manage the tenancy and do the work, we can plan the changeover before the old tenant has even left.

A typical turnaround looks like this:

1. **Check-out inspection** to see exactly what needs doing.
2. **Compliance first:** EICR if it's due, plus any electrical remedial work while the place is empty.
3. **Hidden problems next:** leaks, damp, failed sealant, loose sockets.
4. **Plastering, decorating and any bathroom or kitchen refresh.** A simple bathroom refresh is around a week of work.
5. **Snagging and re-letting**, so the next tenant walks into something that looks cared for.

## Keeping your rentals compliant

Landlords carry a lot of legal responsibility, and the rules keep changing. In England, for example, a rental needs a valid **Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR)** at least every five years, with any remedial work completed. Damp and mould in rented homes are also under more scrutiny than ever.

We keep track of what's due across your properties, and because we're qualified to carry out the EICR and the remedial work ourselves, nothing gets stuck waiting for another firm.

## Older Gloucester properties need the right approach

A lot of Gloucestershire's rental stock is older solid-wall housing: Victorian terraces, cottages and period conversions. Cover those walls in modern cement render or gypsum and you can trap moisture, which leads to exactly the damp complaints landlords dread.

We use period-appropriate materials, including lime, where the building needs it. It's the difference between fixing damp and moving it somewhere else.
