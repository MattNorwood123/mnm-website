Period homes & rentals

Older Homes, Doer-Uppers and Rental Refurbishments

Gloucester is full of older terraces and solid-wall period homes. They need breathable materials like lime plaster and lime mortar. Seal them up with the wrong modern products and you trap moisture and cause damp months later. We use the right materials for the age of your house.

  • Period and solid-wall home renovations
  • Doer-uppers and inherited properties
  • Landlord refurbishments between tenancies
  • Electrical testing and inspection before you start
