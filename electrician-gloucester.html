EICR Electrical Certificates

An Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) is an inspection of your home's wiring, consumer unit and fixed electrical installations, resulting in a formal certificate. It's a legal requirement for rental properties in England (due every five years or at each change of tenancy), and most buyers and mortgage lenders now want to see one before exchange. We carry out the inspection, issue the certificate, and if any remedial work is needed we can complete it ourselves — no need to chase a second contractor.

Fuse board upgrades

Older consumer units — especially those with rewireable fuses rather than modern circuit breakers — don't meet current BS 7671 standards and can be flagged on an EICR as a C2 or C1 observation. A modern unit with RCD protection means that if a fault develops on a circuit, only that circuit trips rather than the whole house going dark. If your board is original to the property or uses a mix of old and replacement parts, it's usually worth upgrading. We'll let you know honestly whether yours needs changing before we start any inspection work.

EV charger installation

Home electric vehicle charging is one of the fastest-growing jobs we're taking on. A dedicated charging point on a spur from your consumer unit charges your car significantly faster than a standard 3-pin socket — most home chargers deliver a full charge overnight where a standard socket would take considerably longer. We assess your current board capacity, run the supply to the most practical point on your driveway or garage wall, and install a unit that's ready for smart tariff scheduling if your energy provider supports it. We handle all the electrical work, from board to charger, and the installation is Part P notified as required.

Full rewires

Older Gloucester properties — particularly those that haven't been rewired since the 1970s or 80s — often have wiring that's approaching the end of its safe service life. Rubber-insulated cables, old steel conduit, and outdated earthing arrangements are common findings on EICR inspections of period homes. A full rewire replaces all the fixed wiring in the property and brings everything up to the current wiring regulations. Because we handle plastering and making good ourselves, we can keep the disruption — and the number of people in your home — to a minimum.
