Legal requirement

EICR Certificates for Landlords & Home Sales

An Electrical Installation Condition Report is a formal inspection of your property's fixed wiring, consumer unit and earthing. It's a legal requirement for rental properties in England — due every five years or at each change of tenancy — and most buyers and mortgage lenders now expect to see one before exchange.

We carry out the inspection, issue the certificate, and if any remedial C1 or C2 observations need addressing we can complete that work ourselves, without you chasing a second contractor to come back.

  • Full inspection of fixed electrical installations
  • Certificate issued on completion
  • Remedial work completed in-house
  • Suitable for lettings, sales, and periodic checks

EV Charger Installation in Gloucester

A dedicated home charging point charges your car significantly faster than a standard 3-pin socket — most home chargers deliver a full charge overnight where a standard socket takes far longer. We assess your consumer unit capacity, run the supply cable to the most practical point on your driveway or garage wall, and install a unit ready for smart tariff scheduling.

Everything from the fuse board to the charger head is handled by us. The installation is Part P notified as required, and we'll advise on grant schemes that may apply to your property.

  • Board assessment and capacity check
  • Dedicated circuit run to driveway or garage
  • Smart charger units, tariff-scheduling ready
  • Part P notification included
Older properties

Full Rewires & Fuse Board Upgrades

Many Gloucester properties — particularly those from the 1960s to 1980s — have wiring approaching the end of its safe service life. Rubber-insulated cables, outdated earthing, and consumer units without RCD protection are common EICR findings in older homes.

A full rewire replaces all fixed wiring and brings the property up to current BS 7671 regulations. Because our team handles plastering and making good as well as the electrics, you don't end up with bare channels in the wall waiting for a plasterer to come back weeks later.

  • Full and partial rewires
  • Modern RCD/RCBO consumer unit installation
  • Plastering and making good included
  • Period properties and new builds
