Electrical safety

Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICRs) in Gloucester

An EICR is a formal inspection of a property's fixed wiring, consumer unit and earthing arrangements, carried out by a qualified electrician and resulting in a written report grading any issues found.

Rental properties in England are required to have their electrical installations inspected by a qualified person at least every five years, with a valid report provided to tenants. This is regardless of whether the tenancy changes — the five-year period runs from the date of the last inspection. We'll advise on your current position and issue the certificate on completion.

For home sales, most mortgage lenders and buyers now expect to see a recent EICR. If any C1 or C2 observations need addressing, we can carry out the remedial work ourselves without you chasing a separate contractor.

  • Landlord EICRs (legal requirement every 5 years)
  • Pre-sale and homebuyer inspections
  • Periodic electrical safety checks
  • Remedial work completed in-house

Fuse board upgrades

Consumer Unit Replacement in Gloucester

Older consumer units — particularly those with rewireable fuses rather than modern circuit breakers — often don't meet current BS 7671 standards and are commonly flagged as C2 observations on EICR inspections.

A modern unit with RCD or RCBO protection means that if a fault develops on a circuit, only that circuit trips rather than the whole house losing power. If your board is original to the property or uses a mix of outdated and replacement parts, an upgrade is usually the sensible move. We'll tell you honestly whether yours needs changing before we begin any inspection.

  • Full consumer unit replacement
  • RCD and RCBO protection
  • Brings installation up to BS 7671
  • Making good included

Older properties

House Rewiring in Gloucester

Many Gloucester properties built between the 1960s and 1980s have wiring approaching the end of its safe service life. Rubber-insulated cables, old steel conduit and outdated earthing arrangements are common findings on EICR inspections of period homes.

A full rewire replaces all fixed wiring and brings the property up to current regulations. Because our team handles plastering and making good as well as the electrical work, you don't end up with bare channels in the wall waiting weeks for a plasterer to come back.

  • Full and partial rewires
  • Period and older Gloucester properties
  • Renovation and extension rewiring
  • Plastering and making good included
