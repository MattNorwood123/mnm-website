Landlord Property Management in Gloucester

Most landlords end up with two sets of people: a letting agent who looks after the tenancy, and a list of tradespeople who fix the property. Every repair becomes a chain of phone calls, and the tenant waits while everyone passes the job along.

We do both. Your tenants have one number to call, and the people who answer it are the same people who fix the problem. You get fewer calls, faster repairs and a clear picture of what's happening at each property.

  • Tenant finding and marketing
  • Rent collection and arrears follow-up
  • Regular property inspections
  • Tenancy paperwork kept in order
  • One point of contact for you and your tenants

Compliance

Keeping Your Rentals Compliant

Landlords carry a lot of legal responsibility, and the rules keep changing. Rental properties in England need a valid Electrical Installation Condition Report at least every five years, with any remedial work completed. Damp and mould in rented homes are under more scrutiny than ever.

We keep track of what's due across your properties. Because we're City & Guilds 2391 qualified, we carry out the EICR and the remedial work ourselves, so nothing gets stuck waiting for another firm.

  • EICRs and electrical remedial work
  • Damp and mould dealt with properly, not painted over
  • Period-appropriate materials for older solid-wall homes
  • Certificates and paperwork kept up to date
